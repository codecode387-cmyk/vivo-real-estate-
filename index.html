About us

Twenty years in South Florida commercial real estate.

VIVO Real Estate Group is a South Florida commercial brokerage built around direct principal involvement. We work across industrial, office, retail, and multifamily assets — representing owners, landlords, tenants, and investors from Miami-Dade through Broward and Palm Beach.

Since opening our doors, our team has closed over $1.2B in transactions and represented millions of square feet of warehouse and distribution space. We're not a large, anonymous brokerage — every mandate is handled personally, from first call to closing table, by leadership holding dual SIOR and CCIM designation.

Our offices in Hialeah, Fort Lauderdale, and Coral Gables put us close to the deals that matter, with the market intelligence and relationships to move quickly when it counts.

"Institutional owners need direct, battle-tested principal involvement — that's the difference between an ordinary sale and a historic one."

Carlos Mendoza founded VIVO Real Estate Group and personally oversees every major disposition, corporate relocation, and industrial acquisition the firm handles. He holds dual designation as an SIOR and CCIM — a rare combination in South Florida.

Over two decades, his work has spanned distribution centers, complex 1031 exchanges, and high-visibility land acquisitions across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach.
