About us

Real estate you can trust, from first viewing to final title.

Home Tour Guide Realty is a Nigerian real estate company offering property management, apartment and condo buildings, real estate title and development, and a carefully selected range of properties for sale. We work with home seekers, investors, landlords, and diaspora clients who want a smooth, honest and transparent property experience.

Every property we offer is affordable, authentic, attractive and approved. We guide you through inspection, verification of documents, payment, and handover, so you can buy, rent or invest with complete peace of mind.

Beyond sales, we manage properties on behalf of owners, handling tenants, maintenance and rent collection so your investment keeps performing while you focus on life and business.

Our promise

A home is more than a building. It is a safe, documented investment.

"Anyone can show you a house. We show you a home with the right documents, the right price and the right people behind it."

Our team physically inspects and verifies every property before it is listed. We confirm the title, check the survey and approvals, and make sure the development matches what is promised, so there are no surprises after you pay.

From affordable starter apartments to executive condos and estate land, we help you choose properly, pay safely, and receive genuine documents for what you buy.
